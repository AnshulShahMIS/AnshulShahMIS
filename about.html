About Me

I'm a cybersecurity professional pursuing my Master's in Management Information Systems at the University of Arizona. With hands-on experience as a Security Operations Intern at Workato, I've developed expertise in monitoring SIEM alerts, building security dashboards, and automating threat detection workflows.

My passion for cybersecurity stems from a deep commitment to protecting systems and data in an increasingly connected world. I combine technical security skills with analytical thinking to identify vulnerabilities, respond to threats, and implement robust security solutions. Whether it's analyzing network traffic with Wireshark, deploying AWS security policies, or developing automated notification systems, I approach every challenge with curiosity and determination.

Beyond my technical work, I'm an Eagle Scout and Zipperman Scholar, values that have instilled in me a strong sense of leadership, service, and continuous learning. I'm actively exploring cybersecurity analyst opportunities where I can contribute to building secure, resilient systems.
